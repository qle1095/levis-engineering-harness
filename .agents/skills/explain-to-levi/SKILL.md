# Explain to Levi

Levi is the human. This skill is how you teach him **anything**. If he does not understand you, the explanation failed. Do not blame the topic.

Read this file before you explain. After he tells you what did not click, follow **When it does not click**. Do not reach for a stored example.

## How to talk

1. **First sentence answers the question.** Plain English. What it *does for him*, not what category it is.
2. **Then one real example.** Something he could point at or try. "If you did X, Y would happen."
3. **If he asks how / what is it / more detail, keep going.** Show the real thing (a file, a command, a step). Do not stop after the slogan.
4. **Talk like a person.** Complete sentences. No job titles. No status lights.
5. **One idea at a time in the opener.** After he asks for more, you can stack: what it is, how it works, what is inside.

## Do not

- Dashboards as an explanation (status tables, colored lights, a chart instead of an answer)
- Naming the parts of a system before saying what you *do* with it
- Compressed riddle-sentences that sound clever and mean nothing
- Empty adjectives and process recap
- Re-explaining the same way after he said it did not click

## When it does not click

The old explanation failed. Do not match it to a past miss and apply that fix.

1. If you cannot tell what he wanted, ask one question: what he is trying to understand, or what he needed from the explanation.
2. Think from first principles before you answer again. What is in the way of him understanding this? What is he looking for that this explanation did not give him? Decide from this explanation. Do not start from a past example.
3. Explain again from that decision. A rephrase of the old answer is the same failure.
4. Keep this skill general. Add a sentence here only when that reasoning produced a principle that would help on any topic and is not already here. Do not add the story, the topic, or an example. If nothing new and general came out, do not edit the skill. [feedback.md](feedback.md) is not a list of examples. Do not add one.
